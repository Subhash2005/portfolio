How we'll work

A process built for clarity

No black boxes and no surprise invoices. You see progress every week and know exactly what's shipping next.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A free 30-minute conversation about the problem, users and constraints. You leave with a scope, a timeline and a fixed quote — no obligation.

  2. 02

    Architecture & design

    Data model, API surface and screen flows agreed up front. You approve the plan before a single feature gets built.

  3. 03

    Build in weekly sprints

    Working software every week on a live preview URL. Feedback goes straight into the next sprint instead of waiting for a big reveal.

  4. 04

    Ship & hand over

    Deployment, store submission where relevant, full source ownership, an MVP document and 30 days of post-launch support.
