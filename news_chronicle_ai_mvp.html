News Chronicle AI - MVP Overview

MVP Document · Product specification

Project Overview

News Chronicle AI is a real-time, AI-powered news aggregator and intelligence platform designed with a classic, premium editorial aesthetic. The MVP serves as a centralized hub where users can monitor breaking global news, organize research, and transcribe live video broadcasts into textual analysis on the fly.

Core Features

  • AI Personas: Contextual lenses applied to search queries to tailor news for Students, Employers, or General Consumers.
  • Live TV Capture: Transcribes live YouTube news broadcasts (e.g., BBC News) into rolling, real-time textual alerts.
  • Nova AI Assistant: A conversational AI integrated directly into the "Explore" tab to answer complex questions across multiple news timelines.
  • Folders & Bookmarking: A localized system for users to save, categorize, and organize news articles for later research.
  • Real-time RSS Scraper: Fetches live global news to ensure the AI's data is completely up-to-date.
