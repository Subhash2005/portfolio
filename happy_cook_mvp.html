Happy Cook - MVP Overview

MVP Document · Product specification

Project Overview

Happy Cook is a dual-sided digital food ordering platform that completely digitizes the dining and takeaway experience. It bridges the gap between hungry customers and local hotel owners by providing a seamless, real-time ecosystem.

Core Features

For Customers:

  • Location-Based Discovery: Automatically fetches the user's live GPS coordinates to display only hotels within a 10km radius.
  • Unified Smart Search: A powerful search engine that instantly finds both specific dishes and hotel names in real-time as you type.
  • Frictionless Checkout: Complete Razorpay integration supporting UPI, Cards, and Netbanking.
  • Digital QR Receipts: Upon payment success, a dynamic QR code is generated in the order history. Scanning it immediately reveals verified transaction details and Payment IDs.

For Hotel Owners:

  • Menu Management: Easily add, edit, and categorize dishes directly from their mobile dashboard.
  • Live Order Tracking: Instantly receive and process incoming customer orders.
